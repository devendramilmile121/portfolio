About

I've spent more than seven years on Angular front ends and the .NET APIs behind them, across healthcare, UN and enterprise products, at four companies in Nagpur and Pune.

Most of that work now is micro frontends: splitting monoliths into apps that can ship on their own, building the shared UI libraries that keep them consistent, and putting YARP gateways in front of the services.

Outside the day job I build Samyo, maintain a couple of Angular libraries on npm, and write up what I learn.
